## About

Approvals are the heart of Vaxnori AI: the moment a founder decides whether an agent gets to spend money, file paperwork or publish something public. You'd own that surface.

## What you'd do

- Own the approvals surface end to end - inbox, task summaries, spend limits, undo windows.
- Design the canvas as agent work becomes more parallel: branches, retries, blocked states.
- Run weekly sessions with founders using the product on live businesses, not prototypes.
- Hold the visual system: one type pairing, one accent, hairline structure, no component sprawl.
- Write the interface copy. Words carry more of this product than layout does.

## What we're looking for

- Five or more years designing product, at least two on something people use daily for work.
- You've shipped a surface where a wrong click costs money or time, and can talk about the guardrails you chose.
- Comfortable in the browser - you can push a fix to a real page without waiting on an engineer.
- Strong opinions on density and typography, and the restraint to keep a system small.
- You like talking to customers more than talking about frameworks.

<Callout title="Nice to have">
You've designed for something with real consequences - payments, health, legal, logistics. Or you've run a small business yourself and remember the admin.
</Callout>
