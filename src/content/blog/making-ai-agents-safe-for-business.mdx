Trust is built from small, visible guarantees. This is a sample post – replace it with your own.

## Approvals

Anything that spends money, signs a document or contacts a customer waits for a person.

## Permissions

Each agent only gets the access its job needs – and you can switch it off at any time.

## Monitoring

Every action is logged with who, what and when, so you can always see what happened.
