Most launches stall in the same place: too many small decisions made in the wrong order. This is a sample post – replace it with your own story.

## Day one: name and paperwork

Pick a name you can actually own. Check the domain and trademark before you fall in love with it, then file the company while the logo is still a sketch.

1. Shortlist ten names and screen them.
2. Buy the domain and claim the social handles.
3. File the LLC and open a business bank account.

## Day two: a page people can find

A single clear page beats a perfect site nobody sees. Write one sentence about who it is for, add a way to pay or book, and publish.

## Day three: the first ten conversations

Talk to people before you build more. Every launch we have seen go well had a founder on the phone by the third day.
