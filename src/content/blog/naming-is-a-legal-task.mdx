A great name you cannot use is not a great name. This is a sample post – replace it with your own guide.

## Start with ten, not one

Write a long list, then cut it down to ten that you would be happy to say out loud.

## Screen before you fall in love

1. Search the trademark register in every country you plan to sell in.
2. Check the domains you actually need.
3. Look for confusing names in your own industry.

## Keep the shortlist

The names that survive both checks are your real options. Pick one and move on – the business matters more than the name.
